MagnusFly Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

English

MagnusFly is a Remote variometer app for paraglider towing. The app uses pilot account data and towing telemetry to connect a Pilot phone with a Driver phone.

Data used by the app may include username, name, email, country, password authentication data, session identifiers, VARIO, AGL, pressure, relative altitude, timestamps, and connection status.

This data is sent to the MagnusFly backend over HTTPS and is used to authenticate users, create towing sessions, transmit pilot telemetry to the connected driver, troubleshoot service operation, and protect the service from abuse.

MagnusFly does not sell personal data and does not use advertising SDKs. Data is not shared with third parties except when required to operate hosting, infrastructure, security, or legal compliance.

To request account deletion, data deletion, or privacy support, contact MagnusSolution using the support contact published with the app listing.
