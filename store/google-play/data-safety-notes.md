# Google Play Data Safety Notes

Use these notes to complete the Play Console forms for `com.magnussolution.magnusfly`.

## Privacy policy URL

https://magnussolution.com/magnusfly/privacy.html

## Data collected

Personal info:

- Name: collected for pilot profile/account.
- Email address: collected for pilot profile/account.
- User IDs: username is collected and used so drivers can request pilot data.

App activity / app info:

- App interactions and session status are used to operate towing sessions.

Device or other IDs:

- Session tokens are generated by the backend for driver/pilot session access.

Location:

- Do not declare precise or approximate location for the current Android app unless GPS/location collection is added later. Current telemetry is barometer-derived VARIO/AGL/pressure and does not include latitude/longitude.

Health, financial, contacts, messages, photos/videos/audio/files/calendar:

- Not collected by the current app.

## Purpose

- App functionality.
- Account management.
- Security, fraud prevention, and service reliability.

## Sharing

- Do not declare sale of data.
- Data is not shared with third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
- Hosting/infrastructure processing is used only to operate the MagnusFly backend.

## Security practices

- Data is transmitted over HTTPS.
- Passwords are sent over HTTPS and stored by the backend as password hashes.
- The app does not provide an in-app account deletion workflow yet. Use the privacy/support contact for deletion requests until that feature is implemented.

## Foreground service declaration

The Android app declares `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` and `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC`.

Suggested Play Console explanation:

MagnusFly uses a data sync foreground service while the Pilot is actively transmitting towing telemetry. The service keeps pilot VARIO/AGL sensor data flowing to the connected Driver during a towing session, including when the Pilot switches to another flight app. The service is user initiated from the Pilot transmission screen and can be stopped by the user from the app/notification.
