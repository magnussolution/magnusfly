# MagnusFly

## Short description

Remote variometer for paraglider towing.

## Full description

MagnusFly is a simple Remote variometer for pilots and drivers during paraglider towing.

The driver starts the connection by entering the pilot username. The pilot only accepts the transmission, and the pilot phone sends VARIO and AGL data in real time. The driver follows the data on their own phone and hears variometer audio feedback.

Main features:

- Remote variometer for tracking pilot climb and sink.
- Remote VARIO and AGL transmission.
- Simple flow: the driver starts, the pilot accepts.
- Minimal pilot profile with username, name, email, and country.
- Interface in Portuguese, Spanish, and English.

MagnusFly is designed for towing scenarios where the pilot may keep another flight app open while the driver needs to monitor vertical speed remotely.
